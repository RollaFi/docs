Rolla is financial infrastructure for businesses operating across Africa and beyond: multi-currency wallets, collections through virtual accounts, payouts over local rails, SWIFT, mobile money and stablecoins, and FX between them — all through one API.

## Which are you?

There are two ways to build on Rolla. Both use the **same API and the same endpoints** — the difference is whose money moves.

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Business" icon="briefcase" href="/business/overview">
    **Manage your own money.** Collect payments, hold multi-currency balances, pay suppliers and partners, and convert between currencies — for your own business account.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Platform" icon="building-user" href="/platform/overview">
    **Offer banking to your customers.** For MSBs, fintechs, and financial institutions: onboard your own users, issue them accounts and wallets, and move money on their behalf — white-label, under your brand.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

<Info>
  Not sure? If your API calls only ever touch **your own** wallets, you're a Business. If you onboard **other people or companies** and hold or move money for them, you're a Platform. Platforms use everything in the Business docs too — plus account onboarding and the `X-Account-Id` header.
</Info>
