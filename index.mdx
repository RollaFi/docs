Rolla is financial infrastructure for businesses operating across Africa and beyond. The Developer API gives you programmatic access to everything the Rolla dashboard can do: hold balances in multiple currencies, collect payments through virtual accounts, pay out over local rails, SWIFT, and stablecoins, and convert between currencies at live rates.

## How the pieces fit

<Steps>
  <Step title="Authenticate">
    Create an [API key](/concepts/authentication) in the dashboard and send it in the `X-API-Key` header on every request.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fund a wallet">
    Generate a [virtual account](/guides/accept-deposits) and receive deposits, or fund via your issued account details.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Move money">
    [Withdraw](/api-reference/endpoint/wallet/withdraw) to a beneficiary, [transfer](/api-reference/endpoint/wallet/transfer) to another Rolla business, or [swap](/api-reference/endpoint/wallet/swap) between currencies.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Track everything">
    Subscribe to [webhooks](/api-reference/webhooks/overview) for status changes and reconcile with the [transaction endpoints](/concepts/transactions).
  </Step>
</Steps>
